What MnemoPay actually is

A developer SDK + hosted dashboard. Not a wallet, not a bank, not a money transmitter, not a credit bureau.

  • SDK — Apache-2.0 npm @mnemopay/sdk and PyPI mnemopay. Install free, run offline if you want.
  • Payment rails — Adapters to Stripe, Paystack, Lightning, x402, AP2. We don't custody funds; the underlying rail operator does.
  • Memory + recall — Local SQLite + cosine. No data leaves your process unless you opt in to the hosted brain.
  • Audit chain — Hash-chained JSONL to disk. You own the file. We don't get a copy unless you POST it to our API.
  • Agent Credit Score — A 300-850 reputation score for software agents. Not a consumer FICO score. Not affiliated with Fair Isaac Corporation. Not governed by the FCRA.

EU AI Act Article 12 — what we do about record-keeping

Article 12 of the EU AI Act requires high-risk AI systems to maintain automatic logs of events that enable traceability of system functioning. The MnemoPay SDK ships this primitive by default:

  • AuditChain appends one JSONL record per agent action — input, output, timestamp, agent ID.
  • Each record carries a SHA-256 hash that includes the previous record's hash. Tamper any record, the chain breaks at that record.
  • The log is local-first. You own the file. verify() walks the chain and confirms integrity in one pass.
  • No vendor lock-in. The format is documented; a non-MnemoPay verifier can read your audit log.

This is the evidentiary trail a regulator asks for. We didn't invent the requirement; we shipped the primitive.
